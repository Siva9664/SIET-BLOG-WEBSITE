# SIET CSE Department Launches High-Performance AI Computing Facility

Department of Computer Science & Engineering, SIET.
Date: August 12, 2026.
Establishment of new High-Performance Computing cluster with 8 NVIDIA GPUs for AI research funded by Institutional Research Grant of INR 35 Lakhs. Lead Coordinator: Dr. V. Muralidharan, Professor & Head of CSE. Intended users: Final-year B.E. and M.E. scholars working on autonomous robotics and vision models.

# Hands-on Workshop on Edge AI and TinyML Systems

Event: Hands-on Workshop on Edge AI and TinyML Systems.
Date: July 18-19, 2026.
Organized by: Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering, SIET.
Resource Person: Er. S. Karthik, Senior Embedded Architect at Silico Labs.
Participants: 85 registered undergraduate students from ECE and EEE.
Hands-on kits: ESP32-S3 microcontroller units paired with OV2640 camera sensors.
Project outcome: Each participant deployed an on-device anomaly detection model.
